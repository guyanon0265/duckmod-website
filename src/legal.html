Privacy Policy

This website has no accounts, doesn't ask for your name or email, and doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Like any website, the services that deliver these pages can see technical details such as your IP address and browser when your device requests them. This site uses Google Fonts for text, jsDelivr for Bedrock downloads, and links out to Modrinth and GitHub. Each has its own privacy policy, and we don't control what they collect.
